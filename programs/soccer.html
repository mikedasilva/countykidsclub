Soccer Skills and Drills

A fun, skill-building soccer program focused on developing ball control, passing, dribbling, shooting, and teamwork — while building confidence and a strong foundation in the game.

What kids experience

Progressive drills and small-sided games that build real skills, week over week, in a fun and supportive environment.

⚽

Ball control & dribbling

Close control, quick feet, and dribbling under light pressure — the foundational skills that make the game click and keep it fun.

🎯

Passing & shooting

First touch, accurate passing, and confident shooting, taught through repeatable drills that carry straight into game situations.

🤝

Teamwork & confidence

Communication, positioning, and cheering each other on. Kids learn to play together and back themselves on the ball.

Soccer Skills and Drills FAQ

Common questions about the program.

Does my child need soccer experience?
No. Drills are progressive and designed for every level — from kids trying soccer for the first time to those who already play on a team.
What should my child bring or wear?
Indoor running shoes with non-marking soles, shin guards, comfy clothes to move in, and a water bottle. We provide the soccer balls and equipment.
Can I stay and watch?
Yes — parents and guardians are welcome to stay and watch. If you plan to drop off, please make sure we have your current contact details and arrive promptly for pick-up.
How many kids are in the program?
The program is capped at 20 kids so every child gets plenty of touches on the ball and individual coaching attention.
How do I register and pay?
We use YouthModo for registrations. Visit our registration portal to sign up — payment details are provided during that process.
What if I have a question not covered here?
Email us at countykidsclub@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
