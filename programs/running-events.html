Running Events FAQ

Common questions about this program.

What are Running Events?
One-time fun runs and races for kids that County Kids Club organizes or co-presents, usually alongside an existing community running event. They're separate from our regular ongoing programs like Movement & Fitness or Soccer.
Is there a running event happening right now?
Not at the moment. Check this page for updates, or join our mailing list and we'll let you know as soon as a new event is announced.
Where can I see details about a past event?
Each event gets its own page with full details. See the Past Events list above for links.
Is fundraising required to participate?
It depends on the event, but our running events to date have not required fundraising — just registration. Each event's own page has specifics.
What if I have a question not covered here?
Email us at countykidsclub@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
