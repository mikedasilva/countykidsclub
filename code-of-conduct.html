The Way We Play At County Kids Club

Participant Code of Conduct

County Kids Club is committed to creating a fun, safe, welcoming, and respectful environment where every child has the opportunity to participate, learn new skills, make friends, and enjoy playing in our programs.

Our programs are focused on participation, development, teamwork, and enjoyment. To help make that possible, we ask all participants and families to follow the expectations below.

Participant Expectations

Children participating in our programs are expected to:

  • Treat coaches, other participants, and volunteers with respect.
  • Be kind and inclusive toward other children.
  • Follow reasonable instructions from coaches, particularly those related to safety and participation.
  • Play fairly and demonstrate good sportsmanship.
  • Use appropriate language and behaviour.
  • Respect program equipment and facilities.
  • Allow other children to participate and enjoy the program without unnecessary disruption or interference.

Behaviour that negatively affects the safety, comfort, or participation of others will not be tolerated. This includes, but is not limited to, bullying, intimidation, repeated teasing, aggressive behaviour, physical violence, threatening behaviour, abusive language, or intentionally preventing another child from participating.

We recognize that children are still learning how to manage emotions, interact with others, and participate in group activities. Coaches will use reasonable judgment and age-appropriate approaches when addressing behavioural concerns.

Behavioural Concerns and Removal from Activities

When behaviour becomes disruptive, unsafe, or prevents other children from participating, coaches may intervene and redirect the child.

Depending on the circumstances, seriousness, and frequency of the behaviour, a coach may determine that a participant needs to:

  • Sit out for a portion of an activity or session;
  • Leave the remainder of a session; or
  • Be removed from the program entirely.

These decisions will be made by the coaching team based on what they believe is necessary to maintain a safe, positive, and enjoyable environment for the group.

Removal from the Program

In situations where County Kids Club determines that a participant can no longer safely or appropriately participate in the program, the parent or guardian will be notified.

Any partial reimbursement of program fees will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis by County Kids Club, taking into consideration factors such as the circumstances surrounding the removal and the portion of the program already completed. Any applicable reimbursement will be discussed with the participant's parent or guardian.

Parents and Guardians

We also ask parents and guardians to help us maintain a positive environment by supporting children positively, treating coaches and other families respectfully, and allowing coaches to manage activities and behavioural issues during sessions.

Our goal is to make sure every child has a fair opportunity to participate, learn, and have fun while keeping the program safe and enjoyable for everyone.

Questions about our Code of Conduct?

Reach out to us any time — we're happy to talk through it.
