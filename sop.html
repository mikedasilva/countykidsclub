Quick Reference Guide

Essentials to remember for every session.

Arrive Ready
Be on time. Review the plan, help with setup, check the space and know your role.
Know Your Group
Take attendance. Be aware of any accommodations or key instructions shared by CKC Program Leads.
Ways We Play
At the beginning of each session, remind kids: Kind hands. Listen with our ears and eyes. Ask for help. Have fun!
Keep Kids Supervised
Parents are required to stay inside or just outside of the gym. Never leave children alone in the gym and avoid being alone one-on-one whenever possible.
Keep it Positive
Use short instructions and keep kids moving. Encourage effort, participation and fun.
Adapt & Include
Modify activities when needed so every child has a meaningful way to participate. Use a water break or quick reset if an activity is not working.
Redirect Calmly
If a child’s behaviour becomes significantly disruptive, involve their parent or guardian. Ask them to help redirect, re-engage or sit with their child for a water break or breather.
Safety First
Pause play if an injury occurs or conditions become unsafe. Involve the child’s parent or guardian if they are injured. For a serious injury or emergency, call 911. If a concussion is suspected, the child must not return to the session.
Communication
Be warm and factual with families. Escalate significant concerns or complaints to CKC Program Leads.
At the End
Tidy equipment and return it to the storage closet. Clean up any garbage. Report anything significant that occurred during the session by email to the CKC Program Lead before leaving.

Coaching Approach

  • Lead with encouragement, patience and positive energy.
  • Focus on participation, effort and fun rather than performance or winning.
  • Use age-appropriate expectations and adapt activities so children with different abilities can participate.
  • Model respectful language and behaviour at all times.

Session Plans

  • Session plans are completed in https://countykidsclub.youthmodo.com/admin/activities
  • All session plans should be completed at least 3 days in advance of the session and shared with the coaching group.

Before Each Session

  • Arrive on time (scheduled set-up time).
  • Review the session plan and confirm coaching team.
  • Set up equipment and check the activity space for hazards.
  • Review the participant list and any relevant accommodations or instructions shared by the Program Lead.
  • Know where the first aid kit, emergency exits and washrooms are located.

Check-In, Supervision & Parent Involvement

  • Take attendance at the beginning of every session.
  • Parents are required to stay inside or just outside the gym for the duration of the program.
  • Whenever possible, avoid being alone one-on-one with a child. Use open, observable spaces. Follow the Rule of Two.
  • Do not allow a child to leave the program area without the appropriate parent, guardian or approved adult.

Running the Session

  • Welcome the group and briefly explain what is happening that day.
  • Remind participants of the CKC Ways We Play: Kind Hands, Listen with Eyes and Ears, Ask for Help, Have Fun.
  • Keep instructions short, clear and positive. Demonstrate activities whenever possible.
  • Keep children moving and minimize long periods of waiting or standing in lines.
  • Build in water and rest breaks as appropriate.
  • Watch for children who need extra support, a quieter option or a short break.
  • Finish with a clear wrap-up and positive send-off.

Behaviour and Inclusion

  • Set simple expectations early: listen when needed, be kind, stay in the activity area and keep yourself and others safe.
  • Redirect behaviour calmly and privately whenever possible.
  • Give children an opportunity to reset and rejoin the activity.
  • If behaviour is repeatedly unsafe or significantly disruptive, involve the parent/guardian and Program Lead. As a lead, you can make the decision if a child should no longer participate.
  • Never yell at, shame, threaten or physically punish a child.
  • Refer to the CKC Code of Conduct.

Physical Contact & Boundaries

  • Keep physical contact appropriate, brief and connected to safety, instruction or encouragement.
  • Ask permission where appropriate before hands-on assistance.
  • Only take or share photos with CKC Program Lead for distribution.
  • If a child needs the bathroom, notify their parent and ask that they take them.

Injuries, Illness, Concussion

  • Stop the activity and assess the situation. Provide first aid within your level of training.
  • For a serious or urgent situation, call 911 and notify the parent/guardian and Program Lead.
  • If a concussion is suspected, remove the child from activity immediately and follow Rowan’s Law and Concussion Safety below.
  • Record any significant injury, safety incident or unusual event before leaving the session.

Rowan’s Law and Concussion Safety

County Kids Club follows Ontario’s Rowan’s Law requirements for concussion safety. If a coach suspects that a child may have sustained a concussion, the child must be removed from the activity immediately and their parent or guardian notified. The child should not return to play that day and may only return in accordance with the appropriate return-to-sport process. Coaches should never try to diagnose a concussion themselves. When in doubt, remove the child from play and err on the side of safety. Ontario also requires applicable sport organizations to have concussion awareness, a concussion code of conduct, and removal-from-sport and return-to-sport protocols.

For more information: https://www.ontario.ca/page/rowans-law-concussion-safety

Parent & Guardian Communication

  • Be welcoming, respectful and clear when speaking with families.
  • Share brief, factual information about the child’s session when appropriate.
  • Bring significant concerns, complaints, injuries or sensitive issues to the Program Lead rather than managing them alone.
  • Do not discuss another child or family with parents/guardians.

Coach Conduct

  • Use appropriate language, clothing and behaviour.
  • Use phones only for program needs, attendance, communication or emergencies while children are in your care.
  • Raise any safety, staffing or program concerns with the Program Lead.

End of Session

  • Confirm all children have left or been transferred to the appropriate adult.
  • Collect and store equipment and leave the gym tidy. Take any CKC equipment with you.
  • Report any injury, incident, parent concern or follow-up item to the Program Lead.
