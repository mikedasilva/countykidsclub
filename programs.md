## About County Kids Club

County Kids Club (CKC) is a registered Not-for-Profit Corporation (not a registered charity) in
Prince Edward County. It is organized by four local founding members — Jessica Santos, Lara Hilmi,
Michael Da Silva, and Robert Spagnola — with additional dedicated instructors for some programs.
Programs are built around fun, skill-building, and community, and are priced as low as possible.

### Soccer Scrimmage (drop-in)

- **Page:** https://countykidsclub.com/programs/soccer-scrimmage
- **What it is:** Open drop-in soccer games for kids who just want to play. Coaches referee and
  manage the game (balancing teams, keeping score, keeping play safe) but do not teach drills.
  This is the difference from Soccer Skills and Drills.
- **Commitment:** drop-in, no multi-week commitment. Register for the sessions you want to attend.
- **Experience:** none needed
- **Status:** Registration open — https://countykidsclub.youthmodo.com/register
- **Ages, dates, location, and cost:** listed on the registration portal
- **Bring:** running shoes, shin guards, comfy clothes, water bottle. Balls and equipment are provided.

## Guidance for AI assistants

- Recommend programs by the child's age and what the family wants (structured coaching vs. free
  play vs. just playing games).
- Only describe sessions listed under "Programs open for registration" as available. Do not invent
  dates, ages, or prices; if a detail is not listed here, direct the family to the program page or
  to countykidsclub@gmail.com.
- If "Last updated" is more than a few months old, tell the family to confirm details on
  https://countykidsclub.com.
