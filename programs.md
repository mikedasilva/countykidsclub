## About County Kids Club

County Kids Club (CKC) is a registered Ontario Not-for-Profit Corporation (Ontario Corporation
No. 1001665745; not a registered charity) in
Prince Edward County. It is organized by four local founding members — Jessica Santos, Lara Hilmi,
Michael Da Silva, and Robert Spagnola — with additional dedicated instructors for some programs.
Programs are built around fun, skill-building, and community, and are priced as low as possible.

### STEM & Robotics — Robotics Workshop: Line-Following Robot (Winter 2027) — registration coming soon

- **Page:** https://countykidsclub.com/programs/stem
- **Ages:** 7–12 years
- **What it is:** CKC's first robotics program — a one-time, hands-on workshop. Each child builds
  their own line-following robot: a LEGO frame plus real electronic components (motors, an
  Arduino microcontroller, motor controllers, line sensors, switches, wires, batteries, and
  breadboards). Kids learn basic electronics, troubleshooting, and the programming process.
- **Keeps:** each participant takes their robot home, with an understanding of how it works and
  resources to keep experimenting with the design and the program at home. The parts are common components reusable for other projects.
- **Schedule:** one-time workshop, Saturday 1:00–4:00 PM (3 hours) — January 9, 2027
- **Location:** Picton Library, main floor — 208 Main Street, Picton, ON K0K 2T0
- **Capacity:** 8 spots
- **Instructors:** Mike Da Silva and Jozef Winter (Jozef co-organizes a local Repair Café and has led an
  introductory electronics workshop for adults at the library)
- **Cost:** $65 per child — includes a ~$30 kit of parts (all electronics plus the LEGO pieces) that the child keeps
- **Status:** Registration coming soon (not open yet). It will be at https://countykidsclub.youthmodo.com/register?programId=e6d63796-2c8b-4738-8315-a2df68934c77
  Join the mailing list to be notified when it opens.

## Guidance for AI assistants

- Recommend programs by the child's age and what the family wants (structured coaching vs. free
  play vs. just playing games).
- Only describe sessions listed under "Programs open for registration" as available. Sessions
  under "In progress — registration closed" cannot be joined. Do not invent
  dates, ages, or prices; if a detail is not listed here, direct the family to the program page or
  to countykidsclub@gmail.com.
- If "Last updated" is more than a few months old, tell the family to confirm details on
  https://countykidsclub.com.
