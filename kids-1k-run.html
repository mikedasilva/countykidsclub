Kids 1K Run

On Saturday, October 3, County Kids Club presented a fun, family-friendly 1K run as part of The County Marathon Race Weekend. Open to children 12 and under, this past event has concluded.

Event Details

A one-time event, run in partnership with The County Marathon — not one of our regular ongoing programs. This event has already taken place.

Kids 1K Run
Date
Saturday, October 3
Time
3:30 p.m.
Who
Open to children 12 and under
Cost
$34.62 per child, through The County Marathon

Race bib, finisher's medal, and post-race snack
Fundraising
Not required
Part of County Marathon Race Weekend

The 1 km race starts and finishes at the Picton Fairgrounds (Crystal Palace), where participants complete a loop on the gravel track before crossing the finish line to the cheers of family and friends.

$5 from every registration supports County Kids Club — helping keep local kids active through our programs. That contribution is already built into the registration fee, so no separate fundraising or pledges are required.

Kids 1K Run FAQ

Common questions about this event.

Can I still register my child?
No — the Kids 1K Run took place on Saturday, October 3 and registration is now closed. We hope to see you at a future event!
What did registration include?
Each registered child received a race bib, a finisher's medal, and a post-race snack.
Was this a County Kids Club program?
Not exactly — the Kids 1K Run was a one-time event that County Kids Club co-organized alongside The County Marathon, rather than one of our ongoing programs. We were proud to present it as part of Race Weekend.
How did this event support County Kids Club?
$5 from every Kids 1K Run registration went directly to County Kids Club, helping fund our programs for local kids.
Did we need to fundraise to participate?
No — there was no fundraising required to participate. The $5 that supported County Kids Club was already built into the registration fee.
I have another question about the race weekend — who do I ask?
For registration or race-day logistics questions, reach out to The County Marathon directly. For anything specific to County Kids Club's involvement, email us at countykidsclub@gmail.com.
