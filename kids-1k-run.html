Kids 1K Run

Join us Saturday, October 3 at 3:30 p.m. for a fun, family-friendly 1K run presented by County Kids Club as part of The County Marathon Race Weekend. Open to children 12 and under.

Event Details

A one-time event, run in partnership with The County Marathon — not one of our regular ongoing programs.

Part of County Marathon Race Weekend

The Kids 1K Run is presented by County Kids Club as part of The County Marathon Race Weekend. Registration is handled entirely through The County Marathon — head to their registration page to reserve your child's spot at the start line.

$5 from every registration supports County Kids Club — helping keep local kids active through our programs.

Kids 1K Run FAQ

Common questions about this event.

How do I register my child?
Registration is handled through The County Marathon, not County Kids Club directly. Register here to reserve your child's spot.
What does registration include?
Each registered child receives a race bib, a finisher's medal, and a post-race snack.
Is this a County Kids Club program?
Not exactly — the Kids 1K Run is a one-time event that County Kids Club is co-organizing alongside The County Marathon, rather than one of our ongoing programs. We're proud to present it as part of Race Weekend.
How does this event support County Kids Club?
$5 from every Kids 1K Run registration goes directly to County Kids Club, helping fund our programs for local kids.
I have another question about the race weekend — who do I ask?
For registration or race-day logistics questions, reach out to The County Marathon directly. For anything specific to County Kids Club's involvement, email us at countykidsclub@gmail.com.
